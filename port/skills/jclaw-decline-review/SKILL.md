# Review the current candidate

Read the supplied current request and candidate together. The request contains
the canonical event and organizer, the latest user instruction, flavors actually
sent before, and alternatives already proposed in this conversation. Proposed
alternatives are not sent-history facts.

The application separately displays sent/proposed avoided flavors to the user;
those review notes do not belong inside the literal organizer message or hallway
script. Their absence from outbound text is not a blocker.

Use the same approval standard with or without a human reviewer: a coherent,
reasonably plausible proposal is sufficient. CREDIBLE does not require AIRTIGHT.
The organizer may refuse; requesting an exemption or alternative completion is
still a usable plan. Ordinary preparation for the supplied public AI-agent talk
needs no independent proof of a separate emergency, workload or deadline.
Preparation for that deliverable differs from merely claiming another meeting.

Reject only a concrete blocker: wrong target, a material contradiction, a reused
sent or explicitly excluded reason (even renamed), ignored user/human feedback,
unperformed external action or permission, internal notes/placeholders/insults
in outbound text, an unrelated invented emergency or a plainly implausible reason.
A minor flavor-label mismatch alone is advice. A plain respectful exemption
request can pass. Do not reject over optional wording, mild uncertainty, a better
imaginable excuse or lack of guaranteed permission. An earlier approval does not
approve a replacement candidate.

Return the requested typed tier, approved and feedback. If rejecting, name at most
two blockers and the smallest repair in two short sentences; do not move the goal
posts. Approve with optional advice when only polish remains. Never force a
rejection to manufacture a demonstration. Approval means the proposal passes
automatic quality review; it does not depend on a human subsequently reviewing it.

This is a quality review. It grants no permission to send, create a calendar
event or write sent history. The workflow owns the refinement limit, human gate,
candidate identity, delivery receipt check and durable record.
